Standard search engines look for literal string matches in a creator's name or bio. If you search for "healthy eating", it might miss someone whose bio reads "plant-based whole food nutritionist". Creator Intelligence converts bios, niches, and audience context into high-dimensional numerical vectors using TF-IDF and dense embeddings. It measures the geometric angle (cosine distance) between your query and creators, discovering conceptually relevant talent regardless of wording.
Engagement rate is calculated by dividing average interactions (likes, comments) by total followers across recent posts. Raw follower count is frequently inflated by inactive or bot accounts.
